# Second review implementation

## Progression and reliability

Automatic purchase reserves protect supply-chain inputs. Supplied public works offer an economic route through late eras and prestige, with actual production funding construction and a ×5 boost to earlier resources when complete. Reality forging has finite per-cycle charges and meaningful proportional costs. Invalid queued choices retire; later prerequisites can execute. Nested save validation, durable checkpoints, and catch-up exception recovery protect progress.

The bounded journey harness now rejects per-era overruns, prolonged periods without meaningful progress, and rejected advertised actions. Signal IDs come from the definitions, and equipped relic limits follow earned slots. The legacy balance minima that required an 80-minute minimalist run and fixed late-era waits were replaced by decision-count checks and maximum durations. The checked-in persona baseline was not changed.

## Later civilizations and reusable plans

Research now extends through prestige 13: reversible production routes, relic combinations, faster early build-out with a replayed blueprint, overflow reclamation, relic conservation, and permanently mapped public works. Four additional reconstruction projects restore industry, preserve another relic, and double the construction value of reclaimed overflow. Started and completed districts remain visible across resets.

Plans record completed decisions and operation orders, plus consumer settings, production routes, and relic loadouts. Players can opt into automatic replay after prestige; prerequisites and normal purchase costs still apply. An incompatible remembered branch cannot block later choices. Completed construction stays completed when a relic combination changes.

Both completion-focused natural journeys finish 22 cycles, all nine research unlocks, all seven reconstruction projects, and three equipped relics. Seed 424242 takes 5,710 simulated seconds and 1,111 manual commands; seed 42 takes 5,730 seconds and 1,111 commands. Both demonstrate active relic combinations, inherited relics, replayed purchases, changed supply routes, and restored production in later civilizations. These journeys deliberately finish eligible projects before prestige, while ordinary personas remain free to reset sooner.
